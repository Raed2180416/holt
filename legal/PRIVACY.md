# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-07-31 · Draft for legal review before first sale.**

## The short version

**The holt software collects nothing.** No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no
"anonymous usage statistics", no license check-in. It makes no network calls at all. Your source
code, repository names, file paths, branch names and symbols never leave your machine, because
nothing in holt is capable of sending them.

This is verifiable rather than promised: the source is public, and the absence of network calls
in `src/` is checked on every release.

## What we do hold

Only what you provide in order to buy a plan:

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Billing name, company, email | To issue and deliver your license, and to invoice you | Our license ledger; our payment processor |
| Payment details | To take payment | **Only** with our payment processor — we never see or store card numbers |
| License records (id, tier, seats, issue and expiry dates) | To support you and prevent duplicate issuance | Our license ledger |
| Support correspondence | To answer you | Our email provider |

## What we never hold

Repository contents, file names, symbol names, branch names, worktree paths, commit messages,
IP-address logs of tool usage, or any record that you ran holt at all.

## Sub-processors

- **Stripe** — payment processing and invoicing
- **Resend** — delivery of license emails
- A cloud host for the license service

Each holds only the data described above.

## Retention

License and billing records are kept for as long as required by tax and accounting law
(typically 7 years). Support correspondence is kept for 2 years. Everything else is not
collected, so there is nothing to retain.

## Your rights

Where GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA apply you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your
personal data, and may object to processing. Email **privacy@holt.dev**; we respond within 30
days. Note that we cannot delete records we are legally required to retain for tax purposes.

## International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and the European Union by the sub-processors listed
above under their standard contractual clauses.

## Children

holt is a developer tool and is not directed at anyone under 16.
