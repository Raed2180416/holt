Five distinct marks, one construction language

Each mark is a completely different form — identifiable without a label. The family resemblance is in the construction: same grid, solid fills, same corner radius, negative space as structure. Not the same shape with variations.

The principle
Contrare Research — a square with one corner inverted. The form is against itself. ARC — containment brackets, solid. A harness holds what's inside. CLIDE — a split square, one half offset. The divided interface. Medes — stacked offset layers. Knowledge with depth. Holt — a lock shackle. Protection, immediately. Five forms you could pick out of a lineup blind.

No labels. If you can't tell them apart, the marks failed.

Parent — Contrare Research
Umbrella · Institute
Contrare Research
A square with one corner inverted. The bottom-right corner is pushed inward — the form is against itself. One corner breaks the pattern. That's the whole thesis: contrarian, orthogonal, going against the grain of the shape's own logic. The inverted corner is the contrarian position made geometric.
ARC — AI Research Cohort
Research core
ARC
Containment brackets. Two solid geometric brackets facing each other with a space between — a harness holds and shapes what's inside. The space isn't empty; it's where the work happens. ARC is the structure that contains the process: the checker, the search, the harness. The brackets are thick and solid — this isn't a suggestion of containment, it IS containment.
CLIDE — CLI + IDE
Interface · Tool
CLIDE
A split square with one half offset. Two panels — one solid, one slightly back and lighter. That's the split-pane IDE, the divided terminal, the interface that shows you two things at once. The offset is the split; the opacity difference is the focus. You see this mark and you see a tool with panels.
MEDES — Interactive Textbook
Reader · Knowledge
Medes
Three stacked layers, each offset up and to the right. Pages with depth — or recursive tooltip layers, each revealing the one beneath. The top layer is solid; the ones below fade. That's recursion made visible: you pull on a term, a layer opens, another beneath it, another beneath that. Knowledge that unfolds downward. ARC + Medes = Archimedes: the brackets (ARC) that contain the layers (Medes) of knowledge.
HOLT — Agent Safety Guardrail
Guardrail · Safety
Holt
A lock shackle — the U-shaped guard on a solid base. You see this and you see security, a padlock, protection. No ambiguity. The shackle is the guardrail; the base is the foundation it protects. Holt locks down what matters. The thickest stroke in the system — because safety should look solid, not delicate.
