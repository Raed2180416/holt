The Broken C

A geometric C where one arm breaks pattern and extends straight — asymmetry becomes leverage. The break creates a wedge of negative space: the fulcrum emerges from what's missing, not what's drawn. Solid, machined, not illustrated.

How it combines all four directions
The Wedge: The break creates a sharp diagonal edge — the mark has a blade, not just a shape. The Counter-grid: One arm breaks the expected C pattern — the letterform is "against itself." The Orthogonal C: The letterform IS the brand, constructed from straight lines, not curves. Negative-space fulcrum: The Archimedes concept lives in the gap the break creates — the fulcrum is what's missing, not what's drawn. Each product changes the break. That's the family.
