# Holt brand assets

These assets are the canonical Holt lockup supplied for the launch materials:

- `holt-lockup.png` — the complete lockup, including the standalone `h` and the `holt` wordmark.
- `holt-wordmark.png` — the wordmark for wider layouts.
- `holt-mark.png` — the standalone stylized `h` for icons, favicons and compact navigation.

Use the artwork as supplied on its warm paper background. Keep clear space around it, do not
stretch it, and do not add trademark or copyright markers. The attribution line is **A Contrare
Research Project**; Holt is part of Contrare Research, which is a research project rather than a
company claim. Product and research questions: `research.contrare@outlook.com`.
